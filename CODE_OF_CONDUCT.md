# Code of Conduct

Sutra is built by and for people who value calm, respectful collaboration.

## Our pledge

We make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Sutra.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **devnetraconsultancy@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and in confidence. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may ban contributors, for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
