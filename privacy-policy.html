Privacy Policy for Sutra

Last Updated: November 2026 · Effective Immediately

Zero Telemetry & Local-First Privacy

Sutra is built with a single philosophy: your bookmarks and browsing habits belong exclusively to you. Your library is stored on your device and is never sent to Devnetra Consultancy. We do not track you, run analytics, or show advertisements. The only network requests Sutra makes are for the weather widget and site icons, described in section 3.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal data or usage metrics.

When you use Sutra, all data you create or manage — including website URLs, bookmark names, notes, custom groups, group sorting order, icon sizes, and custom wallpapers — is stored strictly on your local computer using Chrome's client-side storage.

2. Chrome Extension Permissions & Justifications

In adherence with the Chrome Web Store's Principle of Least Privilege, Sutra requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver its core launchpad functionality:

Permission Type Why Sutra Needs It
storage Required Used solely to store your bookmarks, collections, preferences (theme, icon sizing, layout lock), and custom wallpaper locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.
favicon Required Used to read site icons from Google Chrome's built-in internal cache (/_favicon/), which needs no network request.
unlimitedStorage Required Lets a large library and a custom wallpaper be stored on your device without hitting Chrome's default storage limit.
Weather hosts (get.geojs.io, ipapi.co, api.open-meteo.com, wttr.in) Required Used only by the weather widget, as described in section 3.
icons.duckduckgo.com Optional (On-Demand) Requested only if you turn on "Find missing icons online" in Settings, as described in section 3.
bookmarks Optional (On-Demand) Requested only if and when you explicitly click "Import from Chrome" in Settings. Sutra reads your existing Chrome bookmark titles and URLs once to populate your launchpad. Sutra never modifies, overwrites, or transmits your Chrome bookmarks.

3. Network Requests and Third-Party Services

Sutra sends your bookmarks, notes and groups to no one. It makes these requests, each of which reveals your IP address to the service contacted, as any web request does:

  • Weather: To show local weather, Sutra asks get.geojs.io or ipapi.co for an approximate location based on your IP address, then requests the forecast for that location from api.open-meteo.com. If location lookup fails it asks wttr.in instead. Results are cached on your device for 30 minutes.
  • Site icons from the website itself: When Chrome has no saved icon for a site in your library, Sutra requests that site's own icon files (such as /favicon.ico) directly from that website, without sending a referrer. That website can see the request. Icons are remembered per site so this is rarely repeated.
  • Voice search (only when you click the microphone): Sutra uses Chrome's built-in speech recognition. While the microphone button is active, Chrome sends your spoken audio to Google's speech service to turn it into text, under Google's privacy policy. Sutra never records or stores audio, and listening stops after one phrase, on Escape, or when you leave the tab.
  • Online wallpapers (off by default): Only if you turn on "Online photos" in Settings, Sutra downloads random photos from picsum.photos to use as wallpaper, including the next one ahead of time. No information about you or your library is sent with these requests.
  • DuckDuckGo icon lookup (off by default): Only if you turn on "Find missing icons online" in Settings, and only for sites with no other icon, Sutra sends the site's domain name to icons.duckduckgo.com. Private and local network addresses are never sent.

Sutra also contains:

  • No tracking code or analytics: No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or telemetry scripts.
  • No advertising: No affiliate links, sponsor banners, or tracking pixels.
  • No remote code: In strict compliance with Manifest V3, 100% of Sutra's source code, fonts, and assets are packaged locally within the extension.

4. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

Since all data resides in your browser's local profile:

  • Data Export: You can export a complete JSON backup of your bookmarks and settings at any time from the Settings menu.
  • Backup folder (optional): If you choose a folder in Settings › Library, Sutra keeps a JSON copy of your bookmarks and settings in a "Sutra Backups" folder there, updated automatically, with daily copies for the last 7 days. Exports are saved in the same folder. The files stay on your computer; Sutra never uploads them. If that folder is synced by a service such as OneDrive or Google Drive, that service's own terms apply. Choosing Stop ends the saving and leaves the files for you to keep or delete.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual bookmarks or groups anytime. Uninstalling Sutra from Chrome instantly and permanently purges all stored local data.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Sutra does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children or any individuals of any age. Because Sutra collects no user data, it complies with COPPA and global data privacy standards.
